The questions facilities managers ask.

Your current homepage has eight subheadings, which is already more than Thorn or CAM. These are the ones worth adding.

Can you work outside our operating hours?

Yes, and for most commercial installations that is the sensible way to do it. Evenings, weekends and shutdown periods are normal for us.

How quickly can you get to us if something fails?

Phone rather than email if it is urgent. Response depends on where you are and what has happened, and we will tell you honestly on the call rather than promising an hour we cannot hit.

Do you handle the certification and paperwork?

Yes. Certificates are issued on completion as part of the job, not as something you have to chase afterwards.

We have an EICR with remedials on it. Can you price those?

Yes, and line by line so you can see what is genuinely required against what is advisory.

Is solar actually worth it for our building?

It depends on your roof, your supply and how much of your usage is during daylight. We will tell you if the numbers do not work rather than sell you a system anyway.

Do you take on maintenance contracts?

Yes. Planned maintenance is cheaper than reactive call-outs and it is how we would rather work with a site long term.
